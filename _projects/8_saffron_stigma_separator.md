## Problem

Manual saffron stigma separation is labor intensive and limits throughput for growers.

## What I Built

- Designed a mechanized assembly-line concept for saffron stigma separation.
- Built a prototype and validated the workflow for agricultural handling.
- Quantified projected productivity and economics for practical adoption.
