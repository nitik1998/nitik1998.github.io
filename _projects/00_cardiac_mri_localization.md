**Ongoing research at Johns Hopkins Medicine**, with [Dr. Mike Schär](https://profiles.hopkinsmedicine.org/provider/mike-schar/2777094), while I pursue my MSE in Robotics at JHU.

## Problem

Coronary MR imaging requires careful, largely **manual localization and planning** of the coronary arteries before each scan — a slow, expertise-dependent step that introduces operator-to-operator variability and limits throughput.

## What I'm building

- **Deep-learning localization** of the coronary arteries directly from cardiac MRI, toward automated, reproducible scan planning.
- A pipeline for curating and preprocessing cardiac MR studies into model-ready data.
- Evaluation against expert annotations to quantify localization accuracy and robustness across patients.

## Role

**Student Researcher**, Johns Hopkins Medicine — model development, data pipelines, and evaluation, advised by Dr. Mike Schär.

## Stack

PyTorch · MONAI · medical image segmentation/localization · cardiac MRI
