I'm a **robotics researcher and applied-AI engineer** pursuing my **M.S.E. in Robotics** at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/). I work with [Lalithkumar Seenivasan](https://malonecenter.jhu.edu/people/lalithkumar-seenivasan/) at the [ARCADE Lab](https://arcade.cs.jhu.edu/) on **surgical world models**, and previously researched deep learning for **cardiac MRI** with [Dr. Mike Schär](https://profiles.hopkinsmedicine.org/provider/mike-schar/2777094) at [Johns Hopkins Medicine](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/). My work sits at the intersection of **robot learning, perception, generative AI, and medical AI**.

My current research asks how **vision-language-action (VLA) models** see and act in the world. In [**PAVE**]({{ '/projects/0_pave/' | relative_url }}), I study how end-to-end VLA fine-tuning can silently and *class-asymmetrically* erode affordance signals inside foundation encoders (π₀, OpenVLA, DINOv2, SigLIP) — and develop lightweight ways to recover what's lost.

Before Hopkins, I spent ~3 years building **production AI**: medical 3D vision for early cancer detection at [Qure.ai](https://www.qure.ai/), LLM-powered document intelligence as a **founding engineer** at [Osfin.ai](https://www.osfin.ai/) and [Swiffylabs](https://www.swiffylabs.com/), and perception research for autonomous driving — an arc that runs through my [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), and [experience]({{ '/industry-experience/' | relative_url }}).

My robotics journey began at [IIT Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in/), where I studied [Mechanical](https://www.iitk.ac.in/me/) and [Aerospace Engineering](https://www.iitk.ac.in/aero/) and worked with [Prof. Mangal Kothari](https://home.iitk.ac.in/~mangal/) at IGCL — leading Team IGVC was where robotics first became real for me. I also interned with [Prof. K. Madhava Krishna](https://robotics.iiit.ac.in/faculty_mkrishna/) at IIIT-H and collaborated with [Prof. Indranil Saha](https://cse.iitk.ac.in/users/isaha/) at IIT Kanpur.

I'm drawn to **applied research** and **high-impact, early-stage teams** where these ideas meet real-world deployment. Always glad to talk surgical AI, world models, robot learning, perception, or building AI that actually ships.
