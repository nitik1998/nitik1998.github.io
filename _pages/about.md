I'm a **robotics researcher and applied-AI engineer** pursuing my **M.S.E. in Robotics** at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/). At Hopkins, I work with [Lalithkumar Seenivasan](https://malonecenter.jhu.edu/people/lalithkumar-seenivasan/) at the [ARCADE Lab](https://arcade.cs.jhu.edu/) on **surgical world models**, and previously worked as a researcher with [Dr. Mike Schär](https://profiles.hopkinsmedicine.org/provider/mike-schar/2777094) at [Johns Hopkins Medicine](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/) on deep learning for **cardiac MRI**. My work sits at the intersection of **robot learning, perception, generative AI, and medical AI** — where research depth meets real-world systems.

My current research asks how **vision-language-action (VLA) models** see and act in the world. In [**PAVE**]({{ '/projects/0_pave/' | relative_url }}), I study how end-to-end VLA fine-tuning can silently and *class-asymmetrically* erode affordance signals inside foundation encoders such as π₀, OpenVLA, DINOv2, and SigLIP — then develop lightweight adaptation strategies to recover what is lost.

Before Hopkins, I spent around three years building **production AI systems**: medical 3D vision for early cancer detection at [Qure.ai](https://www.qure.ai/), LLM-powered document intelligence as a **founding engineer** at [Osfin.ai](https://www.osfin.ai/) and [Swiffylabs](https://www.swiffylabs.com/), and perception research for autonomous driving. That arc — from classical perception to embodied and generative AI — runs through my [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), and [experience]({{ '/industry-experience/' | relative_url }}).

My robotics journey began in a different world, before the pandemic, at [IIT Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in/), where I majored in [Mechanical Engineering](https://www.iitk.ac.in/me/) and [Aerospace Engineering](https://www.iitk.ac.in/aero/) and worked with [Prof. Mangal Kothari](https://home.iitk.ac.in/~mangal/) at IGCL. Team IGVC, mentored by him, was where robotics first became real for me: perception, planning, controls, field failures, deadlines, and a robot that had to actually move. I was also fortunate to intern at IIIT-H with [Prof. K. Madhava Krishna](https://robotics.iiit.ac.in/faculty_mkrishna/) and collaborate at IIT Kanpur with [Prof. Indranil Saha](https://cse.iitk.ac.in/users/isaha/) — experiences that shaped my interest in autonomy, perception, and reliable intelligent systems.

I'm drawn to **applied research** and **high-impact, early-stage teams** where robot learning, perception, generative models, and medical AI meet real-world deployment. Always glad to talk surgical AI, world models, robot learning, perception, or building AI systems that actually ship.
