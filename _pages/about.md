I'm a **robotics researcher and applied-AI engineer** pursuing my **M.S.E. in Robotics at Johns Hopkins University**, where I research deep learning for **cardiac MRI at Johns Hopkins Medicine**. My work sits where **robot learning, perception, and generative AI** meet — and where research has to survive contact with the real world.

My current research asks how **vision-language-action (VLA) models** see the world. In [**PAVE**]({{ '/projects/0_pave/' | relative_url }}), I show that end-to-end VLA fine-tuning silently — and *class-asymmetrically* — erodes the affordance signal inside foundation encoders (π₀, OpenVLA, DINOv2/SigLIP), then build a lightweight adapter that recovers most of what's lost.

Before Hopkins, I spent ~3 years building **production AI**: medical 3D vision for early cancer detection at [Qure.ai](https://www.qure.ai/), LLM-powered document intelligence as a **founding engineer** at fintechs [Osfin.ai](https://www.osfin.ai/) and [Swiffylabs](https://www.swiffylabs.com/), and perception research for autonomous driving. That arc — from classical perception to embodied and generative AI — runs through my [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), and [experience]({{ '/industry-experience/' | relative_url }}).

I'm drawn to **applied research** and **high-impact, early-stage teams** where research depth meets real-world shipping. Always glad to talk robot learning, perception, generative models, or medical AI.
