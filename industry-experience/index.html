Applied-AI engineer and researcher with ~3 years of full-time experience taking machine learning from research into production — across medical imaging, fintech, and autonomous systems.

I've worked across the full arc of bringing AI into the real world: the founding engineer who turns an open-ended brief into a deployed product, the research scientist who pushes models past the prior state of the art, and the technical lead who aligns engineering, clients, and compliance around delivery. My work spans medical imaging where errors carry clinical weight, regulated fintech where systems must be trustworthy at scale, and autonomous driving where perception has to fail gracefully.

What ties it together is a consistent operating mode: turning ambiguous, high-stakes problems into reliable systems, at the intersection of research rigor and engineering pragmatism — and a research foundation built through my studies at IIT Kanpur and now Johns Hopkins.
