Projects

  • PAVE — Probing Affordance in Vision-Language-Action Encoders

    Do robot-policy (VLA) models keep the affordance signal of their foundation vision encoders? (JHU graduate research, 2026)

    • A frozen DINOv2-large linear probe reaches 0.776 mIoU on UMD Part-Affordance — +10.6 pp over a CVPR’26 dense decoder, with no decoder, fine-tuning, or augmentation
    • Showed end-to-end VLA fine-tuning (π0, π0.5, OpenVLA) degrades affordances class-asymmetrically (cut −27 pp vs. contain −1 pp), generalizing across two VLA families
