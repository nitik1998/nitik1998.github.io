$ cat ~/bio.txt

I’m a robotics researcher and applied-AI engineer pursuing my M.S.E. in Robotics at Johns Hopkins University, where I research deep learning for cardiac MRI at Johns Hopkins Medicine. My work sits where robot learning, perception, and generative AI meet — and where research has to survive contact with the real world.

My current research asks how vision-language-action (VLA) models see the world. In PAVE, I show that end-to-end VLA fine-tuning silently — and class-asymmetrically — erodes the affordance signal inside foundation encoders (π₀, OpenVLA, DINOv2/SigLIP), then build a lightweight adapter that recovers most of what’s lost.

Before Hopkins, I spent ~3 years building production AI: medical 3D vision for early cancer detection at Qure.ai, LLM-powered document intelligence as a founding engineer at fintechs Osfin.ai and Swiffylabs, and perception research for autonomous driving. That arc — from classical perception to embodied and generative AI — runs through my projects, publications, and experience.

I’m drawn to applied research and high-impact, early-stage teams where research depth meets real-world shipping. Always glad to talk robot learning, perception, generative models, or medical AI.
